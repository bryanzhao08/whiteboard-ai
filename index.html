QUICK START

Draw the way you work.

01

Computer camera
Use your computer webcam to trace in the air. Its preview acts like a mirror and the board cursor follows it.

02

iPhone camera
On a Mac, keep your iPhone locked and nearby, choose iPhone camera, then choose Find cameras. The app selects Continuity Camera. On iPhone, this mode uses the rear camera and stays unmirrored.

03

Make a mark
Touch your thumb and index fingertips and move to draw. Separate them to lift the pen immediately. Hold an off-hand fist to undo, or point with its index finger to change color. Keep hands away from your face. Make a two-finger V and move to drag the board; show two open palms and move them apart or together to zoom.

04

Keep the idea
Recognize writing as an editable note, export PNG or PDF, and sign in to sync once cloud setup is complete.

Hand tracking requires camera permission and an internet connection to load the model. Mouse and touch drawing work without a camera.
